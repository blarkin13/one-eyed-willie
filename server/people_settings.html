Parent PIN

Optional. With a parent PIN, a TV or phone on a kid profile needs it to switch to another profile, and changing profiles here needs it too.

Profiles organise your household; they aren't passwords. Anyone at home can pick an adult profile unless it has a PIN.

Watch history backup

Everyone's watch history, profiles, kids' limits and their own settings, backed up to yarr.media every night, encrypted on this server with a recovery code only you have. yarr.media can't read it. No media, files or passwords are included.

Your recovery code

Save it somewhere safe now (a password manager is ideal). It won't be shown again, and without it a backup can't be opened.

Restore from a backup

For a new or reinstalled server: pick a backup from your account and enter its recovery code. It's merged in; newer progress on this server is kept.
