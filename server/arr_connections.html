How this works

Enter the LAN address and existing API key from each media manager's Settings. Your API keys stay on this server. You don't need a new account or a new public port-forward.

Radarr manages movies; Sonarr manages TV series. One-Eyed Willie can send a title to either service after you choose its folder and quality profile. Adding a title does not mean the media is available to play.

Other *arr applications have different content types: Lidarr manages music, Readarr manages books, and Prowlarr manages indexers. They can be integrated separately when those features are supported; connecting them here would not add movie or TV browsing.
