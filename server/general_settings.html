Playback

An eight-second countdown lets you cancel before the next episode starts. Movies are unaffected.

Automatically uses a theme.mp3, theme.m4a, theme.ogg, theme.flac or theme.wav in each title's folder. If none exists and your Plex server is connected in Settings → Connections, One-Eyed Willie can automatically play that title's existing Plex theme. No music is downloaded from third-party soundtrack sites. Browsers may require a remote/key press or click before sound starts.
