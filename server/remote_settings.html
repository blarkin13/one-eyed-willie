The origin hostname uses free IP-based DNS. It is not active until HTTPS is configured and the router forwards port 443 to your NUC.

Finish remote access

  1. Save your public IP and desired public URL above.
  2. Configure HTTPS and a strong password on the NUC.
  3. Forward TCP 443 on Google Wifi to the NUC. Do not forward 8787.
  4. Connect the public entry point to the HTTPS origin.
  5. Test from cellular data before marking remote access working.

Saving these details does not open router ports, deploy a Worker, or expose your server. Connection status will remain pending until remote access is verified.
