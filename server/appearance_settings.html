Appearance & Navigation

Choose what the sidebar shows, customize its icons and set the TV remote selection color.

Icon theme

Choose Font Awesome icons or the original pirate artwork. Clean uses Font Awesome and is the default.

Changes apply immediately to this browser or TV app. Your pirate ship logo stays the same.

TV selection color

Choose the highlight color used by Google TV remotes. Crimson red is the default. This preference is stored on the server and shared by TVs connected to it.

Ambient effects

Add optional animated weather and seasonal particles while browsing. Effects never intercept remote controls or mouse clicks and disappear during video playback.

Changes save automatically and preview immediately. Preferences are shared with connected TVs. Animation is automatically disabled when reduced motion is requested.

Organize sidebar

Choose which destinations are visible and change their order. Home and Settings stay visible; Settings always stays last.
